02 / TERRAIN ENGINE & TAILSCALE

Ready for a closer look.

ClearSkies and its terrain engine are installed on this computer. Open the app to start the engine automatically. Tailscale is optional for connecting your other devices.

01 / THE TERRAIN ENGINE

More detail. A shared place to process it.

What you can doBrowser, without an engineWith your terrain engine
Explore imagery & mapsPublic satellite imagery, basemaps and browser-accessible national 3DEP.The same maps, plus access to providers that require a server.
Inspect detailed elevationRendered national terrain, with overview fallback.Raw USGS 1 m DEM rendering and WA DNR bare-earth elevation where surveys are available.
Prepare areas for laterBrowser caching depends on the device and browser.Resumable offline-area downloads and persistent server-side terrain caches.
Use specialist layersLayers whose providers allow direct browser access.Engine-backed providers such as WA DNR, Macrostrat and SNODAS.

Detail depends on real survey coverage and provider availability. A terrain engine can unlock finer elevation sources; it does not create higher-resolution satellite imagery. Overview data stays labeled as overview.

02 / OPTIONAL REMOTE ACCESS

Take your engine with you.

Tailscale creates an encrypted private network, called a tailnet, between your devices. It lets you reach your own computer securely from elsewhere without opening a public port on your router.

1

Install & sign in

Install Tailscale on this computer and your other desktops, phones or tablets. Sign them into the same tailnet, or have its owner authorize access.

2

Share your engine

Once private sharing is configured on the engine computer, its personal HTTPS engine link can be used by any device on your tailnet permitted by its access rules.

3

Connect in ClearSkies

Open Settings → Secure terrain engine on the other device, enter your engine link and choose Connect. This works on desktops and mobile devices.

Download Tailscale for Windows ↗Official Tailscale website ↗Downloads for other devices ↗

The Windows button uses Tailscale's version-independent latest stable installer URL. You can install Tailscale during setup or come back later. Tailscale is installed separately and sign-in stays with Tailscale.

What happens now

Your local engine works immediately while ClearSkies is running. Keep its computer powered on, awake and online for remote use. Installing Tailscale alone does not publish an engine link. Private sharing must currently be configured manually; automatic Tailscale engine-server setup and link generation are planned for the next app update.

Everything starts locally.

Reopen this guide with F1 or “ClearSkies Setup and Tailscale” in Start.

To keep the app on your taskbar, right-click ClearSkies in Start and choose Pin to taskbar.
